DIY Smart-AC Module for Daikin MTKL50UV16VA (S403)

Revision: ESP32-C3 Super Mini / no IR / galvanically isolated S403 interface

This project controls the Daikin AC over its S403/S21 service interface using ESPHome. The previous Orient Zeno IR fan-blaster hardware has been removed.

High-voltage warning: S403 is non-isolated and is at mains-referenced potential. Pin 10 is approximately 320 V DC and is not part of this circuit. Do not connect or touch pin 10. Work de-energized and discharge the unit before handling.

1. S403 pinout

PinSignalUse
1+14 VModule power input
2JEM-A OUT*Unused
3JEM-A IN*Unused
4TXAC → ESP32, through ADuM1201 channel B
5RXESP32 → AC, through ADuM1201 channel A
6, 7GNDHot-side ground
8, 9NCLeave unused
10~320 V DCNEVER CONNECT

* S403 pin 2/3 functions are JEM-A related on many documented variants; this project deliberately leaves them unused. Verify your exact indoor-unit service connector before wiring.

Before connecting the module, verify the harness on the actual unit: pin 1↔6 should be around 14 V DC, and identify pins 4/5 against the exact unit. If your board differs, stop and re-map before proceeding.

2. ESP32-C3 Super Mini pin mapping

GPIO20/21 are the ESP32-C3 UART0 pins. Because they are dedicated to S21 here, disable the ESPHome hardware logger with logger: baud_rate: 0.

3. Exact ADuM1201 signal mapping

DirectionPath
AC → ESP32S403 pin 4 TX → R1 1k → VIB pin 3 → VOB pin 6 → GPIO20 RX
ESP32 → ACGPIO21 TX → VIA pin 7 → VOA pin 2 → R2 1k → S403 pin 5 RX

For ADuM1201ARZ the exact pinout is: 1=VDD1, 2=VOA, 3=VIB, 4=GND1, 5=GND2, 6=VOB, 7=VIA, 8=VDD2. Side 1 (pins 1–4) is the hot side; side 2 (pins 5–8) is the isolated cold side.

4. Circuit

The hot and cold grounds remain electrically isolated. The ADuM1201 provides the bidirectional digital isolation and the HLK-B0505S-2WR3 provides isolated power. There is no IR circuitry and no JEM-A circuitry in this revision.

5. ESPHome starting configuration

substitutions:
  device_name: daikin-ac

esphome:
  name: ${device_name}

esp32:
  board: esp32-c3-devkitm-1
  framework:
    type: esp-idf

logger:
  baud_rate: 0

external_components:
  - source: github://asund/esphome-daikin-s21@main
    components: [daikin_s21]

uart:
  - id: s21_uart
    tx_pin: GPIO21
    rx_pin: GPIO20
    baud_rate: 2400
    data_bits: 8
    parity: EVEN
    stop_bits: 2

daikin_s21:
  tx_uart: s21_uart
  rx_uart: s21_uart

climate:
  - platform: daikin_s21
    name: Daikin AC
    visual:
      temperature_step: 1.0

status_led:
  pin:
    number: GPIO8
    inverted: true

The S21 link is commonly documented as 2400 baud, 8 data bits, even parity and 2 stop bits. Exact protocol behavior varies by Daikin model.

6. Power and isolation

  • S403 pin 1 +14 V → 200 mA PTC → 1N5819 → SMBJ18A-protected rail → Mini560 5 V buck.
  • Mini560 5 V hot rail powers ADuM1201 VDD1 and the input of the HLK-B0505S-2WR3.
  • ADuM1201 GND1 is connected only to S403 GND_HOT.
  • HLK-B0505S output provides isolated 5 V to the ESP32-C3 Super Mini.
  • ESP32 3V3 powers ADuM1201 VDD2; ESP32 GND is GND_ISO.
  • Place ADuM1201 bypass capacitors directly at its supply pins. Follow the HLK-B0505S datasheet for its input/output filtering and capacitive-load limits.

7. Build sequence

  1. Build and inspect the isolated interface before connecting it to the AC.
  2. Verify the S403 harness and pin orientation independently.
  3. Power the cold side from an isolated bench supply first and verify the ESP32-C3 boots.
  4. Verify both ADuM1201 signal paths with the hot-side source disconnected.
  5. Only after the electrical checks pass, connect the complete module to S403.
  6. Flash ESPHome over USB-C while the module is isolated from the AC.
IR/JEM-A status: intentionally removed/unused. This module has one job: isolated S21 Wi-Fi control of the AC.
