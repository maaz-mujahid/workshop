Presence Lighting — Bedroom

MediumBedroomHome AssistantESP32-C3 Super Mini~₹800–1,200

mmWave presence + time-of-day lighting. The ceiling node and desk node both use the ESP32-C3 Super Mini for a consistent workshop platform. The ceiling node drives the 6 concealed AC bulbs through a 2-channel relay; the desk node power-cycles the Bluetooth LED strip with an AO3400 MOSFET.

How it works

  1. LD2410C continuously reports moving/still presence and distance over UART.
  2. Ceiling node: ESP32-C3 Super Mini runs ESPHome, talks to Home Assistant and drives two relays.
  3. Wall switches stay parked ON and remain the physical maintenance cut-offs. The relays perform normal switching.
  4. Desk node: the ESP32-C3 Super Mini controls an AO3400 low-side MOSFET in the LED strip USB ground path. The strip auto-resumes on power.

Modes

  • Day: presence → cool group; strip off. Empty 3–5 min → all off.
  • Evening: presence → warm group; strip off. Empty → warm off, strip on. Cool group manual-only.
  • Sleep: Sleep Focus ON → all bulbs off + strip on; presence automations disabled.
  • Morning: Sleep Focus OFF → normal Day/Evening behavior.

Install & safety

Safety ritual: both wall switches OFF + main MCB OFF before ceiling wiring. Verify dead. Bench-flash and bench-test the ESP32-C3 ceiling node before it touches mains.
  • Put the ceiling node in a suitable junction box above the false ceiling.
  • Keep the LD2410C sensor at the selected entrance-wall position, away from the ESP32 antenna and AC airflow.
  • Use proper mains-rated connections and enclosure practices.

Platform standard

All ESP32 boards used by workshop projects are now standardized on the ESP32-C3 Super Mini. The presence-lighting BOM uses P0036 twice: one board for the ceiling node and one for the desk node.
